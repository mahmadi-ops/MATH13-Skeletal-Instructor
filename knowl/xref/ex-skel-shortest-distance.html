Example 4.7.15. Shortest Distance to a Plane.

Find the shortest distance from the point \((1,0,-2)\) to the plane \(x + 2y + z = 4\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution. (Write the distance from \((1,0,-2)\) to a general point of the plane using (3.1.1), then use the plane’s equation to eliminate one variable. Minimizing \(d^2\) instead of \(d\) avoids the square root — say why the two have their minimum at the same place. Find the single critical point and confirm with the Hessian, then argue that this local minimum is the absolute one. Finally substitute back to get the distance itself.)
