Example 3.4.12. Computing a vector perpendicular to two other vectors.

Consider the points \(A(1,-1,2)\text{,}\) \(B(2,0,-1)\text{,}\) and \(C(0,-2,3)\text{.}\) Find a unit vector which is orthogonal to both \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution. (Build the two vectors with (3.2.1), cross them, then normalize with (3.2.8). One component of the cross product comes out zero, which is worth noticing before you take the length. There are two unit vectors that work; say what the other one is.)
