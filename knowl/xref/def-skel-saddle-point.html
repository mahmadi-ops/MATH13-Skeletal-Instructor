Definition 4.7.8. Saddle Point.

A differentiable function \(f(x,y)\) has a saddle point at a critical point \((a,b)\) if in every open disk centered at \((a,b)\) there are domain points \((x,y)\) where \(f(a,b) \lt f(x,y)\) and domain points \((x,y)\) where \(f(a,b) \gt f(x,y)\text{.}\) The corresponding point \((a,b,f(a,b))\) on the surface \(z = f(x,y)\) is called a saddle point of the surface.
