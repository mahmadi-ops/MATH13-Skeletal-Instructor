Example 3.6.2. The line through two points.

Find the vector equation and the parametric equations of the line that passes through the points \(P_0(2,0,0)\) and \(P(1,-1,1)\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution. (Neither point is a direction, so first build a vector parallel to the line out of the two of them with (3.2.1).
